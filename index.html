Bachelor's Thesis

1.0 Final grade

“Extraction of Named Entities from Medical Documents Using Large Language Models — Conception, Implementation and Evaluation”

Supervised by Prof. Dr. Peter Loos and Dipl.-Wirtsch.-Ing. Andreas Emrich · IWi at DFKI · Saarland University, 2026

The thesis compares different approaches to Named Entity Recognition for pharmacovigilance (drug safety monitoring). The models were evaluated on PVRadarKG, a dataset with 19 entity types, with the goal of filling a knowledge graph from clinical texts.

  • Fine-tuned encoders (PubMedBERT, BioBERT) achieved the best overall results.
  • Medium-sized open LLMs such as Gemma-3-27B performed similarly to 70B models when their output was structured with frameworks like LangExtract.
  • The zero-shot model GLiNER worked well without task-specific training.
  • A normalization pipeline, hallucination filtering and a risk-weighted Fω score were used to compare the models.
  • NER
  • LLMs
  • PubMedBERT
  • GLiNER
  • LangExtract
  • Knowledge Graphs
  • Pharmacovigilance
